R!OT WALL, tracking panels

These are the printed panels the phones lock onto. They are Girl Riot artwork, and they are also the tracking targets, so they are built for BOTH jobs: dense edge detail, hard black on paper white, and deliberately no repeating pattern, because repetition is what makes feature matching pick the wrong spot. Each panel is unique and carries its own number so it can be hung in the right place.

Print at A2 or larger on MATTE stock. Gloss blows out under stage lights and kills tracking.
